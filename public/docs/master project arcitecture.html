GDG Management Portal

Product Feature Overview

This document highlights all the core features and capabilities of the upcoming GDG Management Portal. It is written in simple, non-technical language to help the PR, Marketing, and Product teams understand exactly what the application does and how it benefits our community.

1. Smart Dashboard & Quick Access

  • Personalized Greetings: The app welcomes users with a personalized message based on the time of day.
  • Community Snapshot: Instantly see how many members are active, how many are alumni, and who is currently on leave.
  • Quick Action Buttons: Smart shortcuts that change based on your role (e.g., normal members see "Apply Leave", while admins see "Assign Task").
  • Global Search: A powerful search bar at the top to instantly find any task, team member, meeting, or document.

2. Tasks & Projects Management

  • My Tasks & Calendar: Members can view their assigned work in a clean list or pinned on a monthly calendar view.
  • Complete Workflow: Members can 'Accept' a task, 'Submit' their work, and mentors can review and provide ratings/feedback.
  • Task History (Audit Trail): A visual timeline showing the entire journey of a task—who created it, when it was assigned, and when it was completed.
  • Workload Analytics: Beautiful graphs on the dashboard showing how much work is distributed across different teams (like Web Dev or App Dev).

3. Meeting & Attendance Hub

  • Smart Meeting Invites: Meetings only show up for the specific batch or domain they are meant for. No spamming everyone!
  • One-Click Join: Seamless integration to open Google Meet (or other platforms) directly from the app.
  • Attendance Tracking: Admins can easily mark who attended the meeting and record reasons for those who were absent.
  • Minutes of Meeting (MOM): Important notes and agendas are saved within the meeting card for everyone to review later.

4. Leaves, Forms & Data Collection

  • Leave Application: Members can apply for leave for a specific date, task, or meeting. Admins can approve or reject these with one tap.
  • Dynamic Forms: Instead of using third-party tools, admins can create custom forms directly in the app to collect data or feedback from members.
  • Export Data: Admins can view all form responses in a detailed table and easily download them as an Excel/CSV file.

5. Team Chat & Collaboration

  • Dedicated Chat Rooms: Real-time group chats divided by domains (e.g., specific chat room for the App Team).
  • File Sharing: Users can drag-and-drop or upload images and documents directly into the chat.
  • Smart Mentions (@): Tag a specific team member in the chat to send them an instant push notification on their phone.
  • Typing Indicators: See when someone is typing a message in real-time, making conversations feel alive.

6. Announcements & Document Hub

  • Instant Alerts: An in-app Notification Bell keeps members updated on new tasks, approved leaves, and upcoming meetings.
  • Community Broadcasts: Admins can post major news and updates that appear directly on everyone's dashboard.
  • Central Repository: A secure vault for essential community documents. Access is strictly controlled so members only see files relevant to their role.

7. Powerful Administration & Control

  • SuperAdmin Dashboard: A bird's-eye view of the entire organization with high-level metrics and controls.
  • Team Directory: A clean, searchable list of all community members and their contact details.
  • Bulk Onboarding: Instead of adding members one-by-one, admins can upload a single CSV file to instantly onboard an entire batch of students.
  • Custom Access Roles: A flexible security system where admins can create custom roles (like 'Mentor' or 'Domain Head') and strictly control what they are allowed to see and do in the portal.
